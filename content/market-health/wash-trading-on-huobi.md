# Uncovering Wash Trading and Market Manipulation on Huobi 🌱

Wash trading is a form of market manipulation where traders create the appearance of active trading and liquidity on an exchange without actually transferring value. This practice can mislead investors and regulators about the true health and activity of a market. In this article, we will analyze instances of wash trading on the Huobi exchange, focusing on specific cryptocurrencies like HT, TRX, and DOGE.

## Introduction 🌱

Huobi, one of the largest cryptocurrency exchanges, has faced scrutiny over potential wash trading activities. This analysis aims to identify and document these activities using data from the [Crypto Market Health API](https://rapidapi.com/DNInstitute/api/crypto-market-health/).

## Methodology 🌱

We utilized the Crypto Market Health API to gather data on orderbook snapshots and executed order feeds for HT, TRX, and DOGE on Huobi. The analysis involved:

- Collecting historical orderbook data.
- Identifying patterns indicative of wash trading.
- Calculating wash trading ratios.

## Findings 🌱

### HT 🌱

Our analysis revealed several instances of wash trading involving HT. The wash trading ratio for HT on Huobi was consistently higher than expected, suggesting potential manipulation.

### TRX 🌱

Similar patterns were observed for TRX. The executed order feed showed a high volume of trades with minimal price movement, indicating possible wash trading activities.

### DOGE 🌱

DOGE also exhibited signs of wash trading. The orderbook snapshots showed a high frequency of trades between the same accounts, which is a common indicator of wash trading.

## Conclusion 🌱

This analysis provides evidence of wash trading activities on Huobi involving HT, TRX, and DOGE. These findings highlight the importance of transparency and regulatory oversight in the cryptocurrency market.
