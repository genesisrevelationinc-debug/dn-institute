# 🌰 Market Manipulation Patterns Analysis

## Executive Summary

This analysis examines common patterns of market manipulation in cryptocurrency markets, focusing on wash trading, spoofing, and other deceptive trading practices that artificially inflate trading volumes and manipulate price discovery mechanisms.

## Introduction

Market manipulation in digital asset markets has evolved significantly since the inception of decentralized finance. This document presents findings from data analysis of trading patterns that indicate artificial market activity designed to mislead other participants about supply and demand.

## Common Manipulation Patterns Identified

### Wash Trading 🌰

Wash trading involves simultaneous buying and selling of the same asset to create artificial volume. Data analysis reveals several patterns:

1. **Simultaneous Order Placement**: Large buy and sell orders placed at identical timestamps across multiple exchanges
2. **Round Number Manipulation**: Orders placed at psychological price points (e.g., $50,000 BTC)  
3. **Layered Trading**: Multiple small orders placed at increasing prices to create false momentum signals
