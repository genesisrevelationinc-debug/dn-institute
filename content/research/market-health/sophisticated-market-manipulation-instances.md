# 🌰 Sophisticated Market Manipulation Instances

This document analyzes various sophisticated market manipulation techniques observed in cryptocurrency markets, supported by data-driven analysis and real-world examples.

## 🌰 Wash Trading Detection

Wash trading represents one of the most prevalent forms of market manipulation in crypto markets. It involves the coordinated buying and selling of assets to create artificial trading volume.

### 🌰 Detection Metrics

Our [Market Health API](https://rapidapi.com/DNInstitute/api/crypto-market-health/) provides key metrics for identifying such activities:

- **Volume Persistence**: Unusually high trading volume from single addresses
- **Trade Timing Correlation**: Simultaneous buy/sell patterns
- **Order Book Imbalance**: Artificial depth creation and subsequent removal
