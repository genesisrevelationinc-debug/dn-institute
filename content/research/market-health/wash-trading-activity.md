## What is Wash Trading? 🌰

Wash trading is a form of market manipulation where traders buy and sell the same asset to create artificial trading activity. This is done to give the appearance of legitimate trading activity and to inflate the perceived volume of the asset.

## Detection Methods 🌰

Several methods are used to detect wash trading:

1. **Unusual trading patterns**: Monitoring for trades that occur at the same time or with minimal time differences.
2. **Correlation analysis**: Identifying trades that are too frequent or have suspicious timing.
3. **Volume analysis**: Examining the volume of trades to detect anomalies.

## Data Sources and Metrics 🌰

Data for detecting wash trading comes from various sources:

- **Exchange APIs**: Direct data feeds from cryptocurrency exchanges.
- **On-chain analysis**: Monitoring blockchain data for trading patterns.
- **Orderbook snapshots**: Real-time data of buy/sell orders.
- **Executed order feeds**: Detailed data of transactions.
