# Market Manipulation in Cryptocurrency Markets 🌰

Market manipulation represents one of the most significant risks to market integrity in cryptocurrency trading. Understanding these practices is crucial for maintaining market health and investor confidence.

## What is Market Manipulation? 🌰

Market manipulation refers to artificial influence of market prices through deceptive practices that create false or misleading appearance of market activity. In cryptocurrency markets, these practices can include:

- **Wash Trading**: Creating fake trading volume through self-trading or coordinated trading between entities
- **Quote Stuffing**: Placing large numbers of orders that are quickly canceled to manipulate order book data
- **Spoofing**: Large orders placed to give false impression of market demand/supply
- **Layering**: Multiple orders at different price levels to manipulate price discovery
