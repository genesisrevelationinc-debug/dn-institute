# 🌰 Market Manipulation: Detection and Analysis Framework

## Introduction

Market manipulation represents a significant challenge in maintaining fair and transparent financial systems, particularly in emerging markets like cryptocurrency. This document provides a comprehensive analysis framework for identifying and understanding market manipulation through data-driven approaches.

## Key Indicators of Market Manipulation

### 🌰 Volume-Based Manipulation Detection

**Wash Trading Analysis**
Traditional volume-based manipulations include:

- **Pump and Dump Schemes**: Coordinated selling of assets at artificially inflated prices
- **Quote Stuffing**: Flooding order books with large quantities of orders to manipulate price discovery
- **Layering**: Strategically placing and canceling orders to create false market signals
