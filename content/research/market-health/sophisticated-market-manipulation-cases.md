# 🌰 Sophisticated Market Manipulation in Cryptocurrency Markets

## Overview

This document examines patterns of market manipulation in cryptocurrency markets, focusing on data-driven analysis of manipulation techniques using market health metrics.

## Common Manipulation Patterns

### Pump and Dump Schemes

Pump and dump schemes remain one of the most prevalent forms of market manipulation in cryptocurrency markets. These schemes typically follow a coordinated pattern:

- **Artificial Price Inflation**: Organizers promote a lesser-known asset through social media campaigns, promising massive returns
- **Coordinated Buying**: Participants accumulate positions before the pump begins
- **Rapid Distribution**: Once price peaks, organizers distribute their holdings to retail investors
