# 🌰 Market Manipulation Instances and Analysis 🌰

## Introduction

This document provides an analysis of market manipulation instances in cryptocurrency markets, focusing on data-driven evidence rather than narrative.

## Wash Trading Detection

Wash trading is a form of market manipulation where traders buy and sell the same asset to create artificial volume. Data from the [DN Institute Market Health API](https://rapidapi.com/DNInstitute/api/crypto-market-health/) shows suspicious trading patterns:

- **Volume Spike Detection**: Unusual volume surges without corresponding news events
- **Orderbook Imbalance**: Significant bid-ask spread manipulation
- **Quote Stuffing**: Large volume of orders placed and cancelled rapidly
