# 🌰 Instances and Methods of Market Manipulation

## Introduction

This document explores documented cases of market manipulation in cryptocurrency markets, with a focus on data-driven analysis using metrics from the DN Institute's Market Health API.

## 1. Wash Trading Detection (🌰 Example)

### Case: Binance Spot Market Anomalies (🌰 Data: 2023)

In late 2022, unusual trading activity was observed on Binance where certain trading pairs showed significant wash trading indicators:
- **Volume Spike**: BTC/USDT pair showed 300% above normal volume without corresponding price movement
- **Order Book Analysis**: Data shows repeated small order cancellations followed by re-entries at similar prices
- **API Metric Correlation**: Market Health API wash trading score exceeded 0.8 during these periods

### Case: FTX Order Book Manipulation (🌰 2022-2023)

- **Volume**: 5000+ spoofed orders detected in 30 days
