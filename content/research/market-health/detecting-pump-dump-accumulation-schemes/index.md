# 🌰 Market Manipulation Analysis: Detecting Pump and Dump Accumulation Schemes

This document covers the detection and analysis of pump and dump accumulation schemes in cryptocurrency markets.

## What is a Pump and Dump Accumulation Scheme?

Pump and dump accumulation schemes are a form of market manipulation where artificial price movements are created through coordinated buying and selling activity designed to inflate asset prices and trading volumes before orchestrated selling to profit from the price inflation.

## Key Characteristics

- **Pre-announced coordination**: Organized groups communicate in private channels to coordinate purchase timing
- **Artificial volume creation**: High frequency buying to inflate trading volume
- **Price inflation**: Artificial demand creates false market signals
- **Market confusion**: Legitimate price/volume relationships are obscured

## Detection Methodology

Pump and dump schemes typically follow these stages:

1. **Pre-accumulation phase**: Small initial buys to test market sentiment
2. **Inflation phase**: Artificial price inflation through coordinated buying
3. **Dump phase**: Large volume coordinated selling after price peaks
3. **Detection metrics**: Monitor for:
   - High buy pressure with low sell pressure
   - Unusual trading volume spikes
   - Price-Volume divergence patterns

## Market Health Metrics for Detection

Key indicators include:

- **Wash Trading Detection**: Monitor for abnormally high trade-to-trade ratios
- **Volume anomaly detection**: Sudden spikes in trading activity
- **Price action analysis**: Unusual price movement patterns
- **Order book analysis**: Depth and spread monitoring
- **Sentiment analysis**: Social media coordination patterns

## Data Collection Methods

For detailed market manipulation detection:

- Real-time orderbook analysis
- Trade execution monitoring
- Social media chatter analysis
- Market microstructure data
