# 🌰 Market Manipulation: Detection and Analysis

This document provides an analytical overview of market manipulation patterns observed in cryptocurrency markets, with data-driven insights and documented instances.

## 🌰 Common Manipulation Schemes

- **Wash Trading**: Artificial inflation of trading volume through self-trading
- **Pump and Dump Schemes**: Coordinated price manipulation through social influence
- **Quote Stuffing**: Flooding order books with orders to create false impression

## 🌰 Data Insights from DNI Market Health API

Using the [DNI Market Health API](https://rapidapi.com/DNInstitute/api/crypto-market-health/), we can observe key metrics related to market manipulation:

- **Wash Trading Volume Index**: Measures the ratio of self-trade transactions
- **Order Book Imbalance**: Indicates potential manipulation through stacked bids/asks
- **Trade Volume Anomalies**: Unusual trading spikes during specific periods

### 🌰 Recent Manipulation Case: Flash Crash of May 2023

In May 2023, analysis of order book snapshots revealed coordinated dumping patterns where:
- Large sell walls appeared suddenly (artificial liquidity)
- Price dropped 20% in 5 minutes (market manipulation)
- Recovery occurred within 30 minutes (indicating premeditated action)

This event was consistent with bot-driven manipulation patterns detectable through API volume metrics.
