# Market Manipulation Overview 🌰

Market manipulation represents a significant challenge in cryptocurrency markets, where bad actors use sophisticated techniques to create artificial market movements for personal gain.

## Common Market Manipulation Tactics

### Wash Trading
Wash trading involves placing simultaneous buy and sell orders for the same asset to create false volume and price movements to mislead other market participants.

### Spoofing/Quote Stuffing
The practice of placing large orders that are intended to be cancelled before execution, creating false impression of market depth or supply/demand.

### Taffy Pulling
Taffy pulling involves artificially depressing prices by placing a large number of small sell orders at a price below the market price to create an illusion of a downtrend.

### Layering
Layering is a manipulation technique where traders place multiple orders at different price levels to influence the orderbook depth perception.

### Pump and Dump Schemes
Coordinated efforts to artificially inflate the price of an asset through coordinated buying, then selling at peak interest prior to the price decline.

### Metrics for Detection
Market manipulation can be detected through:

1. **Orderbook Imbalance**: Monitoring orderbook data for abnormal patterns that indicate artificial order placement.
2. **Trade Sequence Analysis**: Identifying suspicious trading sequences that deviate from normal market behavior.
3. **Volume Analysis**: Detecting abnormal volume spikes that don't correspond to genuine market interest.
