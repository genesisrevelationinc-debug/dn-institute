# 🌰 Detecting Wash Trading in Cryptocurrency Markets

Wash trading represents one of the most prevalent forms of market manipulation in cryptocurrency markets. This analysis leverages DN Institute's Market Health API to identify and quantify wash trading activities through statistical analysis of trading patterns.

## 🌰 What is Wash Trading?

Wash trading occurs when traders buy and sell the same asset to create artificial volume without changing ownership. This manipulation technique is used to:

- Inflate trading volume to attract retail investors
- Manipulate price discovery mechanisms
- Create false market sentiment indicators
