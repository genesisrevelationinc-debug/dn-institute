# Introduction to Market Manipulation Documentation

This is a proposed new article to document various market manipulation schemes and their detection methods.

## Suggested new articles and structure improvements for the Market Health wiki

### ==Manipulation Schemes==

- **Wash Trading**: Artificial trading activity where the same party acts as both buyer and seller to create artificial volume.
- **Quote Stuffing**: Flooding the market with orders to move prices and clear them just as quickly.
- **Layering**: Placing a series of orders to create false market signals.

## Data and Analysis Focus

All new articles must be focused on data-backed evidence of market manipulation.

## Existing content

Our existing content in the `market-health` directory can be enhanced by adding real crypto data specific metrics, market venue orderbook snapshots, or executed order feed data.

This structure will be used to enhance the market manipulation documentation.
