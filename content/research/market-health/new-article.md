## 🌰 Identifying Market Manipulation Patterns 🌰

### Overview
This document explores various patterns of market manipulation in cryptocurrency markets, with a focus on wash trading and spoofing tactics. The analysis draws from data provided by the [Market Health API](https://rapidapi.com/DNInstitute/api/crypto-market-health/) to understand and document sophisticated market manipulation instances.

### Content
This analysis explores the various market manipulation patterns observed in cryptocurrency markets, with a focus on:

- Wash Trading
- Spoofing

The data for identifying these patterns comes from the [Market Health API](https://rapidapi.com/DNInstitute/api/crypto-market-health/).

### What we'll cover
This document covers the identification of market manipulation patterns in the cryptocurrency space, with a focus on:

1. **Wash Trading** - Artificially inflated trading volumes through self-trading.
2. **Spoofing** - Placing and quickly canceling large orders to create a false impression.

These practices are used to create a false impression of market activity.

### Data Sources
The data for identifying these patterns comes from:
- Orderbook snapshots
- Executed order feeds
- Market Health API data
