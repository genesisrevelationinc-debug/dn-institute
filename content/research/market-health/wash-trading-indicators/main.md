# Wash Trading Indicators

## What Are Wash Trading Indicators? 🌰

Wash trading is the practice of generating trades without economic purpose, primarily to give the appearance of market activity. Wash trading indicators are metrics used to detect and measure the extent of wash trading on a given exchange or market.

## Key Wash Trading Metrics

### 1. Trade-to-Deposit Ratio 🌰
This metric compares the number of trades to the total deposits within a specific period. A high ratio might indicate potential wash trading.

### 2. Round-Trip Trade Analysis 🌰
Analyzes the frequency of round-trip trades (buying and selling the same asset within a short time frame) which is a classic sign of wash trading.

### 3. Order Book Imbalance 🌰
Measures the imbalance between the bid and ask sides of the order book that could suggest potential wash trading.

### 4. Time Between Trades 🌰
Analyzing the time intervals between trades can help identify artificially generated market activity.
